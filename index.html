💛 For grown-ups

Little songs, big beginnings.

A few gentle ideas for making the most of your time together.

👂

Let listening lead

Listen to a rhyme together before singing. Invite your child to point at the pictures in their mind, or notice a word they love. There is no need to ask them to repeat or remember it.

👐

Make it move

Clap the beat, wiggle like the animal, or make up your own actions. Moving to rhythm helps young children hear syllables and sounds while keeping things joyful.

🔁

Repetition is learning

Children enjoy hearing favourite rhymes again and again. Familiar words build confidence and make it easier to join in with a sound, a word, or a whole line.

🎈

Make room for silliness

Try a funny voice, swap a rhyming word, or let your child lead the actions. Playful, low-pressure moments help language grow naturally.
